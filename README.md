## My Favorite Series: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

**Genre:** Action / Adventure / Supernatural
**Creator:** Hirohiko Araki
**First published:** 1987

*A multi-generational saga about the Joestar family and their battles against supernatural evil.*

### Parts 1-6
| Part | Title | Protagonist | Setting |
|------|-------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | Phantom Blood | Jonathan Joestar | England, 1880s |
| 2 | Battle Tendency | Joseph Joestar | USA, Mexico, Italy, 1938 |
| 3 | Stardust Crusaders | Jotaro Kujo | Japan to Egypt, 1989 |
| 4 | Diamond Is Unbreakable | Josuke Higashikata | Morioh, Japan, 1999 |
| 5 | Golden Wind | Giorno Giovanna | Italy, 2001 |
| 6 | Stone Ocean | Jolyne Cujoh | Florida, USA, 2011 |

### Why I Love It
- Creative and unique battles, from Hamon to Stands
- A new cast and setting in every part
- Iconic poses, art style, and music references

### Story Highlights
1. **Parts 1-2:** The Hamon era, where the Joestar bloodline faces Dio Brando and the Pillar Men
2. **Parts 3-4:** The Stand era begins, with a road trip to Egypt and a mystery in Morioh
3. **Parts 5-6:** A mafia gang story in Italy and a prison break in Florida
